From shortlist to parcel

BoonBuy finds and product links

When comparing Weidian sneaker listings, Taobao clothing links or 1688 product variants, save the seller listing URL, selected colorway and date checked. If a product link stops working, search by model code and compare the replacement listing’s photos and options before adding it to your shortlist.

BoonBuy sizing guide

Use a sneaker size chart in centimeters and compare foot length with the seller’s sizing instructions. For clothing, compare hoodie chest width, T-shirt body length, jacket sleeve length and shorts waist measurements with similar pieces you own. Check whether the seller lists flat measurements or full circumference.

BoonBuy QC photos

Ask for an outsole close-up, a readable shoe size label and a photo of both shoes together. For a jersey, check crest placement and print alignment. For a cap or bracelet, check the back closure or clasp. Warehouse QC photos can help confirm the variant and visible condition.

BoonBuy shipping estimate

Record actual parcel weight and packed box dimensions. Compare volumetric weight, domestic delivery charges, service fees and international shipping options for the same destination. For deliveries to the US, UK, Canada or the EU, use the destination country and postal code when available. Ask for a new quote if you change the packaging.

Clothing and accessory references

Frequently asked questions

What is a BoonBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product index that helps shoppers organize finds by category and compare seller listings before ordering. This guide points to CAFANS categories.

Do I need Excel to browse this guide?

No. Open a category directly in your browser. You can keep your own shortlist in a spreadsheet or a notes app.

What should a QC photo show?

Look for the selected color and size label, plus clear close-ups of seams, soles, fasteners or other details that matter for that item.

How do I compare parcel shipping estimates?

Use the same destination, packed weight and parcel dimensions for each estimate. Check the carrier's billing method and any fees listed for that route.

Where do the category links go?

All ten category links open their corresponding sections on CAFANS. This is an independent guide with no official affiliation with BoonBuy or the pictured brands.
